Language Trainer: licences

The plugin's code and its letter charts (plugins-src/language-trainer/*.py
other than data_*.py, and tools/build_language_trainer_data.py) are under the
MIT licence in plugins/LICENSE.txt.

Words (the WORDS tables in data_*.py) come from the translation tables of
English Wiktionary, https://en.wiktionary.org, by Wiktionary's contributors,
read through the extracts at https://kaikki.org. A few entries were replaced
by hand, and romanizations were adjusted to match the letter charts. They are
under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode
Changed versions of these tables must be shared under the same licence.

Sentences (the SENTENCES tables in data_*.py) come from Tatoeba,
https://tatoeba.org, by its contributors, under the Creative Commons
Attribution 2.0 France licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/legalcode
Each sentence keeps its Tatoeba number; its author and history are at
https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<number>.
