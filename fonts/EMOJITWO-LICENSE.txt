Emojitwo — emojitwo.ttf
=======================

emojitwo.ttf is a COLRv0 colour font assembled by the Emoji-COLRv0 project from
the Emojitwo emoji artwork set (version 2.3.0).

  Font build:  https://github.com/Emoji-COLRv0/Emoji-COLRv0
  Artwork:     https://github.com/EmojiTwo/emojitwo  (originally EmojiOne 2.2)
