Blobmoji — blobmoji.ttf
========================

blobmoji.ttf is a CBDT/CBLC colour bitmap emoji font built from the Blobmoji artwork
(Blobmoji 17, Release 1) by David Berdik (DavidBerdik/blobmoji2), originally based on
Google's Noto Emoji and C1710's Blobmoji.

  Font build:  https://github.com/DavidBerdik/blobmoji2
  Original:    https://github.com/C1710/blobmoji
               https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji
