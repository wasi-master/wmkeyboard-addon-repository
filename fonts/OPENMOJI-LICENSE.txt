OpenMoji Black — openmoji-black.ttf
===================================

openmoji-black.ttf is the "OpenMoji-black-glyf" build (release 17.0.0): the
monochrome outline set, as plain TrueType glyphs rather than a colour font, so
emoji draw in the keyboard's own text colour.
