Twemoji — twemoji.ttf
=====================

twemoji.ttf is a COLRv0 colour font built from the Twemoji artwork
(Twemoji 17.0.2) by the Nerixyz/twemoji-colr-font project — release v0.0.2,
asset Twemoji_GlyfColr0.ttf.

  Font build:  https://github.com/Nerixyz/twemoji-colr-font
  Artwork:     https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji  (originally Twitter, Inc.)
