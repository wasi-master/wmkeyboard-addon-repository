Twemoji Mozilla — twemoji.ttf
=============================

twemoji.ttf is the "Twemoji Mozilla" build (version 0.7.0), a COLRv0 colour
font assembled by the mozilla/twemoji-colr project from the Twemoji artwork.

  Font build:  https://github.com/mozilla/twemoji-colr
  Artwork:     https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji  (originally Twitter, Inc.)
